FAQ

Common questions.

Something else on your mind? Send a message and we'll answer directly.

What does Roland Tech do?

Roland Tech is an e-commerce agency. We design and build online stores on Shopify, Wix and Webflow, then help them grow with Facebook Ads, SEO, graphic design and product research.

Which platforms do you build online stores on?

We build on Shopify, Wix (including Wix Studio) and Webflow. Shopify suits most product stores, Wix suits bookings and service businesses, and Webflow suits brands that want a custom, content-rich site.

Can you move my store to a different platform?

Yes. We help brands move between platforms such as Wix, Webflow and Shopify. Send us your current store link and we'll plan the move with you before any work starts.

Do you run Facebook Ads for online stores?

Yes. We set up the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, test ad creatives and scale budgets around your real margins, so ads bring sales and not just clicks.

How long does a project take?

Most Shopify or Wix builds take 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope. Webflow projects with custom CMS work can take a little longer. You get a clear timeline before any work starts.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. All work is remote, and we work with clients across time zones. We can talk over email, WhatsApp or whatever tool suits you.

I already have a store. Can you just fix it?

Yes, that's a common request. Send us your store link and what isn't working, and we'll tell you honestly whether it needs a rebuild or a few targeted fixes.

Who will I work with?

A dedicated Roland Tech team: specialists in e-commerce, websites, marketing and graphics, depending on what your project needs. Roland is your main point of contact the whole way through.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a support window after launch for fixes. Ongoing work like ads, SEO and updates can continue after that on a separate plan.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the platform and scope. A simple Wix site costs less than a custom Shopify build with app integrations. Tell us about your project through the form and we'll send a clear quote.
